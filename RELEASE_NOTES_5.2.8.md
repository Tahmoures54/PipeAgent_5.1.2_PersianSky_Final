# PipeAgent 5.2.8 — Teamwork shell cleanup

## Why this revision

The turquoise top bar was crowding the workspace: mixed-size actions, a Support button next to Alerts/Refresh/Users, and a live-status chip that duplicated the status bar.

## Changes

- Menu and top-bar label **Users** is now **Teamwork** (roster and access levels).
- Top-bar actions sit left of a stretch, same 36 px height, left-aligned text: Teamwork and Alerts only.
- Contact Support (WhatsApp) lives under **Help**. Refresh stays on File (F5). The online chip is gone; signed-in name/role remains on the right.
