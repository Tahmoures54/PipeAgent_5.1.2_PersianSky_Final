# PipeAgent Go-to-Market Blueprint

## Ideal Customer Profile

Primary: EPC contractors and construction management organizations executing complex piping packages.

Secondary: fabrication yards, piping subcontractors, commissioning teams and owner project controls groups.

## Initial wedge

Sell a **90-day controlled Pilot** around one Area/System or one Work Front family. Baseline:

- weld production
- fit-up cycle time
- NDT queue
- repair rate
- blocked Work Fronts
- evidence completeness
- turnover readiness

At the end of the pilot, produce the Executive Value Report and convert the customer to Professional or Enterprise.

## Expansion path

Pilot → Professional → Field Operations → Intelligence → Digital Turnover → Enterprise Integration → Multi-project Portfolio.

## Why customers buy

The product promise is not "record more data". It is:

> Know what is happening, understand what is at risk, and know what should happen next.

## Commercial guardrails

No dark patterns, no invented ROI, no fabricated AI certainty and no automatic approval of controlled engineering/QC actions.
