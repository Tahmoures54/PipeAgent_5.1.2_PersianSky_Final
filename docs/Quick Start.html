The Rule to Remember

Don't just record what happened.

Use PipeAgent to understand what is happening, what is blocked, what is at risk and what should happen next.

Field Pattern

QR → Scan → Fit-up → Weld → Evidence → QC → NDT → Repair → Acceptance → Test → Turnover

When Something Goes Wrong

  1. Check the affected Work Front.
  2. Review its constraints.
  3. Open the Execution Graph.
  4. Review Smart Insights.
  5. Check predicted downstream impacts.
  6. Follow the recommended Next Best Action.
  7. Let the authorized responsible person approve the action.

First Management Check

At the beginning of every shift, review:

  • Blocked Work Fronts
  • Ready but unassigned work
  • NDT backlog
  • Repair trend
  • Testing readiness
  • Turnover evidence gaps
  • Predictive risks
