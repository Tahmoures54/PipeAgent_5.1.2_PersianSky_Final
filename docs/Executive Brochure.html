The Problem

Piping execution generates enormous amounts of information.

Weld registers, inspection requests, spreadsheets, Work Fronts, photographs, documents, punch lists, test packages and turnover records often live in separate systems.

The result is delayed visibility and late decisions.

The PipeAgent Approach

One connected execution picture.

PipeAgent connects field execution, quality, engineering evidence and turnover into a persistent Execution Graph.
