**Know What’s Next. Control What Matters.**  
Piping Execution Operating System

Supporting line: **Connect the field. Predict the risk. Control the execution.**

## Revenue model

PipeAgent is designed as a B2B enterprise product. The commercial model is intentionally **not priced per weld**. Production volume should increase customer value, not the customer's penalty for using the system.

Revenue layers:

1. **Platform subscription** — project/organization annual subscription.
2. **Field Operations** — active field users/devices for QR, offline capture, evidence and synchronization.
3. **Intelligence** — predictive bottlenecks, Quality Intelligence, Work Front Autopilot, impact propagation and Next Best Action.
4. **Digital Turnover** — project or turnover-package add-on.
5. **Enterprise Integrations** — setup + annual maintenance for ERP, scheduling, DMS, BIM and identity systems.
6. **Professional Services** — implementation, migration, configuration, training and go-live support.

## Product tiers

### Pilot
For evaluation and small controlled deployments. Limited Field Operations; intelligence and enterprise features are optional.

### Professional
For small and mid-size EPC projects. Includes core platform, Field Operations, Intelligence and Digital Turnover capabilities.

### Enterprise
For large EPC and multi-project organizations. Includes the full platform, Intelligence, Turnover, enterprise integrations, multi-project architecture and SSO/RBAC capabilities.

## Value selling

The commercial conversation should be based on measurable operational value rather than feature count. PipeAgent can produce an **Executive Value Report** using project evidence such as repair events, document volume, constraints, open impacts and prediction data quality.

The value engine is intentionally conservative. Any dollar figure is an **indicative opportunity**, not a guaranteed saving. Customer-specific economics must be verified before external or contractual use.

## Pricing governance

- Never claim a guaranteed ROI without verified baseline data.
- Never fabricate avoided delays or savings.
- Keep pricing configuration separate from construction business logic.
- Preserve human approval for engineering, QC and contractual decisions.
- Keep enterprise connectors optional and configuration-driven.
