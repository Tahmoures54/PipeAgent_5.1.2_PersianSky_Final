# PipeAgent UX & Product Psychology — 2.7

PipeAgent uses behavioral design to increase adoption and purchase intent without dark patterns, fake urgency, hidden fees, or deceptive claims.

## Principles applied

1. **Immediate value** — the dashboard answers “What should I do next?” before showing secondary detail.
2. **Progressive disclosure** — advanced controls stay available but the first screen emphasizes the next operational action.
3. **Recognition over recall** — Line → ISO → Spool → Weld selections reuse existing project data.
4. **Loss avoidance through visibility** — Ready-but-unassigned fronts, blockers, idle risk, and incomplete turnover evidence are surfaced early.
5. **Momentum** — Quick Entry, Work Front dispatch, and Excel import are prominent because they produce visible progress quickly.
6. **Trust** — AI recommendations show evidence and recommendations separately; imports are read-only until confirmation; official reports require an approval step.
7. **Friction reduction** — Excel templates, validation previews, bulk import, reusable context, and field workflows reduce repetitive typing.
8. **Confidence before commitment** — licensing and purchase messaging should explain operational value and trial/activation state without pressure tactics.

## Conversion-oriented product surfaces

- Splash: communicates a focused execution outcome rather than a generic loading message.
- Login: explains the product promise in one sentence and establishes trust.
- Dashboard: “Today’s Execution Focus” makes the next action obvious.
- Quick Entry: reduces repeated project-context selection.
- Excel Import Wizard: previews errors and duplicates before any database write.
- AI: prioritizes actions from project evidence instead of presenting an opaque score.
- Turnover: shows completeness so teams can see the remaining work needed before handover.

## What PipeAgent must not do

- No fake scarcity or countdowns.
- No disguised advertising.
- No automatic purchase/activation.
- No misleading productivity claims.
- No hiding export or data-access options to force retention.

The goal is sustainable purchase intent: users should buy because PipeAgent makes field execution faster, clearer, and more controllable.
