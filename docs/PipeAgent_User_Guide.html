Welcome to PipeAgent

PipeAgent connects engineering, construction, quality, testing, documents and turnover into one execution operating system for EPC and industrial piping projects.

The operating principle is simple: capture the event, understand the constraint, predict the impact and act before the delay becomes expensive.

End-to-End Execution

Engineering→Work Front→Fit-up→Welding→NDT→Repair→Testing→Punch→Turnover
1
Project Setup

Create Areas, Systems, Lines, ISO references, Spools and master data. Use Excel import where appropriate.

2

3

4
Quality & Completion

Digital Turnover

  1. Review blocked and at-risk Work Fronts.
  2. Check ready work without assigned resources.
  3. Review NDT backlog and repair trend.
  4. Check test-package readiness.
  5. Review turnover evidence gaps.
  6. Read predictive alerts and impact propagation.
  7. Assign and track the Next Best Actions.
